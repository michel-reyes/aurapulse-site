AuraPulse

A personal wellness app for iPhone · private beta, personal use

Privacy PolicyTerms of Service

AuraPulse compares each night's temperature, resting heart rate, heart rate variability and breathing rate with your own recent nights, alongside your cycle and the context you log, such as alcohol, a hard workout, stress or a short night. It reads data you choose to share from Apple Health and, if you connect it, from your Oura account.

Your data stays on your iPhone. AuraPulse has no servers, no accounts and no analytics or advertising tools. Everything is processed on your device.

AuraPulse is a general wellness app. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease or condition.
