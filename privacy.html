Privacy Policy

HomeTerms of Service
In short: AuraPulse runs entirely on your iPhone. It has no servers, collects nothing about you, and never sells, shares or uploads your health data.

Who we are

AuraPulse is an iPhone app developed by Michel Reyes and currently used privately by its developer. Contact: mreyesh85@gmail.com.

What data the app uses

  • Apple Health, only the types you allow: sleep, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, respiratory rate, sleeping wrist temperature, steps, active energy, workouts, and the cycle and symptom entries you have logged.
  • Oura, only if you connect your Oura account: daily sleep and readiness summaries, sleep periods, heart rate, activity, workouts and tags, within the permissions you grant on Oura's consent screen.
  • What you enter in the app: context tags (for example alcohol or a hard workout), symptoms, cycle entries and profile choices.

How it is used

The data is used only to show you how each night compares with your own baseline, inside the app on your iPhone. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any purpose other than showing you your own information.

Where it is stored

  • All processing and storage happen on your iPhone, protected by iOS data protection. The app's data is excluded from iCloud and device backups.
  • The Oura access token is kept in the iPhone Keychain, on this device only.
  • AuraPulse has no servers and no accounts, and it includes no analytics, crash-reporting, tracking or advertising SDKs.

Sharing

AuraPulse does not sell, rent or share your data with anyone. Data leaves the app only when you choose to: saving your logs back to Apple Health (off unless you turn it on), exporting a data file, or sharing a PDF summary you create.

Oura data

  • Oura data is used only to show your own metrics to you and is never used to train, fine-tune or improve any AI or machine-learning model.
  • It is kept on the device only as long as needed to compute your baselines (at most about 200 days) and is not combined with other data to identify anyone.
  • When you disconnect Oura in the app, the app asks Oura to revoke its access and deletes the Oura data and anything calculated from it on your iPhone immediately.

Apple Health data

Data from Apple Health is never used for advertising or data mining, never shared with third parties, and is never stored in iCloud by AuraPulse.

Your choices

  • Change Apple Health access at any time in the Health app (Profile › Apps › AuraPulse).
  • Disconnect Oura in AuraPulse (You › Data sources) or revoke access from your Oura account.
  • Delete everything with You › Delete all AuraPulse data, or by deleting the app.

Children

AuraPulse is not intended for anyone under 18.

Not medical advice

AuraPulse is a general wellness app. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease or condition.
